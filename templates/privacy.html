Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Spendly ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and password when you register for an account. We also collect the expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may automatically collect limited technical information, such as browser type and access times, to help us operate and maintain the Service.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to provide, maintain, and improve the Service — for example, to authenticate your account, display your expense history, and generate summaries and category breakdowns. We may also use your data to communicate with you about your account or important changes to the Service. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account information and expense records are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell your personal or financial data to third parties. We may rely on trusted third-party providers for infrastructure needs such as hosting, and these providers only process data on our behalf and under appropriate confidentiality obligations. We do not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on our website. We're happy to help.
